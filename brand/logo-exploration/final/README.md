# Atlas Production Asset

`atlas-app-icon.svg` is the production master for the Hercules app icon.

- Canvas: 1024 × 1024
- Background: Hercules Ink `#26241F`
- Mark: Hercules Ivory `#F2EFE8`
- Construction: selected Atlas monogram with a rising center bridge
- Effects: none

The raster files in `Hercules/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset` and `Hercules/Assets.xcassets/HerculesLogo.imageset` are generated from this master at their exact catalog sizes.
